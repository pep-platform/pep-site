One platform for the whole partnership

{{brand}} takes a partnership from its first objective to its renewal case. The Strategy Module sets what the deal is for and builds the plan to deliver it. The Measurement Module tracks performance against that plan and builds the evidence for what happens next.

Whether you're a brand renewing a flagship deal or a rights holder pitching a new title partner, {{brand}} gives you the plan and the data to make it pay off.

They're just the objectives. Set them whenever.

Less than half of sponsorships begin with clearly defined commercial objectives. The industry treats effectiveness as a measurement problem, but the best data stack in the world won't rescue a badly planned sponsorship.

If objectives aren't agreed before signing, activation has nothing to aim at, the campaign sits apart from the rest of the brand's communications and the media budget caps reach. That's an effectiveness problem, and it's set on day one, long before anyone opens a dashboard.

So {{brand}} starts where the value is won: the plan.
