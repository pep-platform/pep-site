Most sponsorships die of exposure.

{{brand}} started with a question. The sponsorship sector is stuck measuring exposure and media value, so could software apply the latest effectiveness research and marketing science to any partnership, quickly, without watering down the thinking?

Over months of development and testing, Box Count's strategy workflow was encoded and its outputs refined until they met the standard Rory holds his own client work to. Data Agents' measurement frameworks, built for brands defending their marketing spend to a board, sit underneath the Measurement Module: models built on the client's own data, with every assumption visible.

The method keeps learning. Every piece of research Rory publishes and every framework he develops becomes a new knowledge module, built on the established principles of marketing science, so every recommendation rests on the evidence of what works.

Where the people come in

The software does the analysis. On managed accounts, Rory and Simon add the judgement: which objectives a partnership can realistically hit, what the data is actually saying, what to change mid-season and how to make the renewal case.
