It's only £20m over 5 years. Who needs experts?

Software is quick to build these days. The method behind it isn't. pep runs on the frameworks Rory and Simon use with their own clients, drawing on around 50 years of sponsorship strategy and measurement experience between them.

Anyone can ask a chatbot for a sponsorship strategy. What comes back is generic, because the model has never sat in a renewal meeting. pep is built on tested strategy and measurement methods, it's updated every time new research is published, and the people who built it are on hand whenever a decision needs an expert.
