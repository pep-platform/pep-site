Acme Shaving should plan for a total investment of £8.5m to £12m per season, with activation set at 2 to 3 times the rights fee to turn the club's reach into measurable penetration and share gains among men 35 to 54.

Rights fee£2.5m to £4m

Category-exclusive grooming rights. No incumbent grooming partner, so first-mover pricing is available. Per Binet and Davis (IPA, 2026), rights without activation weight behind them do not move commercial metrics.

Activation budget£6m to £8m

Set at 2 to 3 times the rights fee to buy the reach and frequency needed to shift mental availability among men 35 to 54. Fame campaigns need enough weight to earn media and visibility at scale.

Total investment£8.5m to £12m

All-in season one: rights, creative and media, measurement and the launch platform. Year one builds the baseline; years two and three compound penetration and share.

Activation : rights2 to 3x

The rights fee buys access; activation generates the repeated exposure that shifts mental availability.

Rights / activation split30 / 70

In a Fame-led model the creative and media spend does the commercial work. The club is the context; activation is the engine.

Measurement~5% of total

Funded from the outset, not retrospectively, per SEF best practice: brand tracking, a matched-market incrementality design and a sales baseline.

Word-of-mouth advocacy

Ahead

Likely cause: football fan culture carries strong peer recommendation. Acme Shaving's presence across broadcast and digital is reaching men who already talk about the game, turning exposure into recommendation among the 35 to 54 target.

Do next

Capture and redistribute organic advocacy content through the club's own channels to carry recommendation into the off-season.

Evidence: SEF

Buyer penetration

On track

Likely cause: the twelve-month purchase rate among UK men 35 to 54 is moving against baseline, consistent with an early phase where brand effects come before sales effects.

Do next

Run an exposed-versus-unexposed purchase comparison at the next read to confirm partnership contact, not seasonal category demand, is driving the shift.

Evidence: SEF

Qualified audience reached

On track

Likely cause: verified impressions to UK men 35 to 54 are landing as planned. Reach is an input, not an effect, so this confirms the media plan is running, not that it is working.

Do next

Check reach is spread across enough fixtures to build frequency. If it is bunched in one or two matches, redistribute across the season.
